# CLARITY — THE ESSENTIALS
**The highest-leverage focus file. Ten moves. Distilled from the Reflect study + the NeuroFOCUS lesson. Read this before designing anything.**

---

1. **One sentence rules the surface.** Every page argues exactly one sentence ("Think better with Reflect" / "Get moved, get paid"). If an element doesn't serve the sentence, it goes.

2. **One glowing body per sky.** A surface earns ONE radiant thing — the eclipse, the mint, the payable amount. Light it like the only sun. Everything else stays material.

3. **Promise the reader, not the product.** Headlines are verb-first and about *them*: "Think better." "Move first." "Get paid in DASH." Never "Our powerful platform…"

4. **Space louder than content.** The gap between sections should exceed the sections. If it feels busy, there are too many things — never too little glue.

5. **Count to three.** Three focal things per screen, maximum. At four, change the design — split the screen, or switch material (that's the NeuroFOCUS lesson: density inverts a style into slop).

6. **Show, then state, then stop.** Proof is a working thing, not an adjective. One product shot beats four paragraphs; one flat guarantee ("we never hold funds — we couldn't if we wanted to") beats a trust page.

7. **One plan, one price, one line.** Simplicity of the offer is itself a feature. Every fee fits in a sentence or it's too complicated to ship.

8. **Two words per feature.** Breadth is a glyph + two words. Paragraphs are reserved for the moment of decision.

9. **Full-strength ink, always.** Hierarchy comes from size, weight, and case — never from fading text. Calm without grey: that's the proof of craft.

10. **Calm is the luxury signal.** Nothing spins, nothing shouts. One signature motion per surface; the rest is stillness. Restraint reads as intelligence — and intelligence is the brand.

*Ring rule: end where you began — repeat the promise at the door.*
