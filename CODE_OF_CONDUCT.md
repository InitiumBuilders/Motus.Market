# Code of conduct

## The short version

This project is about people making public commitments and then helping each other keep them. Behave
here the way that idea deserves.

## Our standards

Things that make this place work:

- Assuming good faith, especially when someone is wrong.
- Being specific instead of dismissive. "This breaks on mobile at 375px" is a contribution;
  "this is bad" is not.
- Welcoming people who are new to this, to open source, or to systems thinking. Everyone was.
- Accepting a decision you disagreed with once it has been made, and saying so plainly if you still
  think it is wrong.

Things that will not be tolerated:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption, or bad-faith participation.
- Using this project's surfaces to misrepresent, impersonate, or defraud anyone.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, reviews — and to
anyone representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abuse through
**[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/InitiumBuilders/Motus.Market/security/advisories/new)**
on this repository, or by opening an issue titled `conduct report` containing no details, after
which you will be given a private channel. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, and contributions that violate this document, and may
temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they judge inappropriate. Where a
warning is enough, a warning is what you will get; repeated or severe behaviour will not get one.

Enforcement decisions are made by the maintainers of
[Initium.Builders](https://github.com/InitiumBuilders).
